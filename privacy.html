Privacy policy

GTA6 Clipper · last updated November 17, 2026

Who uses this app

GTA6 Clipper is a private tool. Its only users are the owner's own YouTube, Gmail, TikTok and Twitch accounts. It is not offered to anyone else.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: uploads videos to the owner's channels, and reads those channels' own video list and analytics.
  • Gmail: sends emails the owner has approved, and reads the inbox to spot platform notices and replies.
  • TikTok: uploads videos to the owner's accounts and reads basic profile and video stats.
  • Twitch: checks whether approved streamers are live, and reads public chat activity.

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to make, schedule and post clips and to measure how they perform. Sign-in tokens and stats are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

GTA6 Clipper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from Google account permissions, TikTok's and Twitch's connected-apps settings, or by deleting the app's local token files.
